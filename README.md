# Banking Technology Risk Analytics

### Executive Risk Analytics | Portfolio Project

**Project by:** Augusta Imo-Jack  
**Focus:** IT Risk Management | GRC | Audit Analytics | Data Visualization

## Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates how banking technology risk and audit findings can be analyzed to identify risk exposure, monitor remediation performance, and support management decision-making.

Using a simulated banking technology risk dataset, I developed a Tableau executive risk analytics story to communicate key risk indicators, finding trends, and remediation priorities.

## Business Problem

Organizations need clear visibility into technology risks, unresolved audit findings, and remediation progress. Without effective reporting, management may struggle to identify high-priority exposures and track corrective actions.

This project explores how risk analytics can help address those challenges.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze technology risks across key risk categories.
- Identify high-rated open findings.
- Evaluate the aging of unresolved findings.
- Monitor remediation progress and overdue items.
- Present actionable recommendations through executive-level visualizations.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Tableau:** Data visualization, dashboards, and executive risk reporting.

These results reflect the simulated dataset used in this portfolio project.

## Executive Risk Insights

**High-Rated Open Findings:** 11 of the 25 assessed risks are high-rated and open, representing 44% of the assessed risk population.

**Finding Aging:** Privileged Access has the highest average open-finding age at 32.5 days, followed by Disaster Recovery at 29 days.

**Remediation Performance:** 22 of the 25 assessed risks have remediation statuses other than Completed, representing 88%.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize the review and remediation of high-rated open findings.
2. Investigate aging findings, particularly in Privileged Access and Disaster Recovery.
3. Validate remediation ownership, target dates, and escalation procedures.
4. Monitor remediation performance through regularly updated risk dashboards.
5. Strengthen management reporting using clearly defined and validated risk metrics.
